Actionable Business Recommendations

+₹3.7 Lakhs/yr Lift
Weekend Combo Bundles

Weekend order volume is high, but AOV drops significantly to ₹280. To counteract this, introduce "Weekend Family Packs" or "Cuisine Combos" targeting larger groups. Bundle a main dish, side, and dessert for ₹450 to pull the AOV up on Saturdays and Sundays.

+₹5.5 Lakhs/yr Lift
Bangalore Logistics Redeployment

Bangalore lags in On-Time Delivery (~62% vs Mumbai's ~84%) due to heavy traffic. Redeploy delivery hubs closer to high-density clusters and establish a dynamic routing SLA algorithm that assigns shorter-range deliveries to bicycles or local riders during dinner peak hours.

+₹4.0 Lakhs/yr Recovery
"Active Rider" Retention Incentives

35% of cancellations occur due to delivery partners being "unavailable" during lunch and dinner peaks. Establish a peak-hour multiplier incentive structure: riders completing 5+ orders during peak hours get a 1.5x payout boost, increasing active delivery supply when customer demand surges.

+₹2.8 Lakhs/yr Lift
Cohort-Targeted Winback Campaigns

Cohort retention drops from ~38% in Month 1 to under 15% in Month 3. Deploy automatic push notifications and coupon codes ("We Miss You - ₹100 Off") to customers entering their Month 3 post-registration window to combat the steep operational churn curve.

Case Study Methodology & Setup

Problem Statement

Food delivery startups operate on razor-thin margins and are highly dependent on operational efficiency, customer repeat rates, and regional demand dynamics. This project is a complete analytical deep-dive into the performance of ZippyBites, a synthetic food delivery platform operating in 6 Indian metro cities. The objective is to identify operational bottlenecks (such as logistics delays, cancellation factors, and driver shortages), study customer ordering patterns (retention drop-offs, pricing dynamics), and build a predictive forecasting model to support senior executive decision-making.

Synthetic Data Generation Details

A robust synthetic dataset was programmatically generated using Python. The dataset comprises 25,000+ orders over a 12-month period (June 2025 – May 2026). It simulates realistic relationships:

  • Weekend Pricing Patterns: Programmed AOV drops on weekends (₹280) compared to weekdays (₹380) representing price-sensitive discount shoppers.
  • Logistics Bottlenecks: City-specific traffic variables (e.g., Bangalore has a 1.6x traffic multiplier leading to longer delivery times and lower ratings).
  • Customer Cohorts: Assigned unique customer IDs with random power-law order distributions to calculate accurate monthly customer retention cohorts.
  • Cancellation Vectors: Modeled dinner-rush cancellations caused by rider unavailability and restaurant rejections.

Data Limitations & Transparency Disclosure

Synthetic Dataset Profile: This case study utilizes programmatically generated synthetic data. In the commercial food delivery space, transactional and logistics logs are highly proprietary corporate assets and not publicly available. Designing synthetic data with realistic constraints (such as weekend discount shocks and geographical traffic congestion multipliers) enables testing of structural analytics pipelines without compromising IP boundaries, reflecting professional field judgment.

Predictive Forecasting Model

Using the monthly aggregated revenue, we implemented a simple linear regression model in Python to project future revenues:

y = mx + c

Where y is projected monthly revenue, x is the month index (0-12), m represents the growth rate (slope), and c represents the baseline intercept.

The calculated values (slope, intercept, and a 1-month forecast for June 2026) are displayed on the Predictive Sandbox tab, coupled with an interactive business simulator that displays revenue projections in real-time.

Model Limitations & Trade-offs: This single-variable linear model acts as a baseline trend line. It does not account for holiday seasonality (e.g., Diwali or Christmas spikes) or promotional marketing campaign spend edits. In a production scenario, a time-series framework like **SARIMA** or **Prophet** would be deployed to overlay periodic seasonality indices.

Future Technical Roadmap

To evolve this case study from a static single-page portfolio dashboard to a production-ready analytics system, the following engineering upgrades are planned:

  • Database Migration: Transition data storage from static CSV/JSON files to an index-optimized relational database (PostgreSQL) or document store (MongoDB). Create a backend API (FastAPI or Express.js) to query, paginate, and filter large datasets dynamically, reducing initial browser load memory footprint.
  • Seasonal Forecasting Models: Implement a secondary forecasting worker using SARIMA or Facebook Prophet via Python to capture complex Indian seasonal factors (monsoons, Diwali shopping peaks, weekend shifts) rather than baseline linear regression.
  • Rider Availability ML Model: Incorporate a predictive logistics module using XGBoost or Reinforcement Learning to suggest real-time pricing and optimal rider payout multipliers for the 8 PM to 10 PM peak hours, minimizing order cancellations.
